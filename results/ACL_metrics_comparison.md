### Key take-aways

1. **Policy Recall 0.93 (+21 pp over RAG, +30 pp over few-shot):** the 6-pass extraction pipeline with PDF-native heading detection recovers policies that single-pass approaches miss, while maintaining high precision (0.91).
2. **Conflict F1 82.4 (+11.2 over semantic similarity, +13.7 over Z3-only):** the neuro-symbolic hybrid catches conflicts the LLM misses while avoiding Z3 encoding gaps that plague purely symbolic methods.
